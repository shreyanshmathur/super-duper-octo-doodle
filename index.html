💡 How This Works — Plain English
AI converts solar plant data into financial intelligence. It predicts how much power the plant will generate by analysing weather forecasts, satellite irradiance data, cloud cover patterns, and historical plant performance. This AI forecast is used to schedule power delivery to consumers — DISCOMs, open-access industries, captive users, and power exchanges. When actual generation matches the schedule closely, deviation penalties are avoided. When they diverge, penalties are deducted from revenue. The dashboard tracks all of this in real time, showing the developer exactly how much energy was generated, how much was scheduled, what revenue was earned from each consumer, what penalties were incurred, and how much money AI helped save by reducing forecast error.
